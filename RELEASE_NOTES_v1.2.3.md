# Little Steps v1.2.3 — The Wondergarden Adventures + Cozy Cottage 🌷

Our first complete five-chapter storybook adventure is ready to explore!

Join Kristie, a curious little robot, as she learns to find her way through a garden filled with flowers, muddy paths, woodland friends and starlight. Play manually with arrow keys or WASD, compare BFS, Dijkstra and A*, or train Kristie with tabular Q-Learning.

### In this release

- Five playable Season One chapters with distinct stories, objectives and a closing scene.
- Optional guided tutorial, animated Kristie, manual navigation, challenge stars, XP and Robot Diary.
- A cozy **Little Garden Corner** with real saved collection progress.
- **Season 1.5: Kristie's Cozy Cottage** — switch between soft daytime Outdoor and Indoor Loft views.
- Fixes for the cottage view buttons and storybook artwork presentation.
- English project README and a Chinese player's guide.

**Note:** The Cozy Cottage currently offers two illustrated home views; furniture placement and free decorating are planned for later. Season Two and its island World Map are also future concepts, not included here.
