# Wooden boat visual update

This preview preserves the original Little Steps game and changes the Season Two boat art:

- Replaces the red paper boat on the Chapter 3–5 storybook pages with hand-painted natural wooden boats.
- Keeps Kristie, the white cat, charcoal cat and golden puppy in the story illustrations.
- Makes all 9 advanced chapter image files exactly **714 × 718 pixels**, matching the Chapter 1/2 story-image asset dimensions. The full landscape scene is preserved inside each square artwork with softly faded margins.

- Corrects the Chapter 3 text that still mentioned a red boat.
- Does not otherwise modify algorithms or progress.
