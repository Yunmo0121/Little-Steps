# Little Steps v2.0.0 — Season Two Complete

**Release:** Season Two — The Enchanted Isles  
**Play:** https://yunmo0121.github.io/Little-Steps/  
**Repository:** https://github.com/Yunmo0121/Little-Steps

A new chapter in Kristie's story is ready: **five playable island adventures** with illustrated openings and tabular reinforcement-learning experiments. This release includes both complete seasons and her Cozy Cottage home between them.

## What is new

- **Five island chapters:** Lantern Bay, Tidal Island, Misty Reef, Windward Passage and Starharbor.
- **Interactive learning:** independent Q-Learning and SARSA training, α/γ/ε controls, seeded experiments, learning charts, action-value inspection, and playable sea grids.
- **Different RL questions on each island:** tides and WAIT actions; exploration in fog; stochastic gusts; collecting three beacons before returning to harbour.
- **Illustrated map and story pages:** a gentler coastal watercolour palette, wooden-boat artwork, chapter-specific icons, and a three-page narrative for each island.
- **One connected game:** pink storybook main menu, World Book, Season One's five garden chapters, Robot Diary, Collection and Cozy Cottage.

## Scope & limitations

This is a personal educational simulation, not a realistic marine-navigation model. Misty Reef's visual fog does not constitute a complete POMDP. Season Three is only a future concept. Cozy Cottage supports viewing, not free decorating. Progress is saved locally in the browser.

## Validation

Seven included Node.js test scripts passed during packaging; JavaScript syntax checks passed. Browser interaction checks are still recommended before public sharing, especially on mobile and after deploying GitHub Pages.

**Next story:** At the end of Starharbor, Kristie discovers a mysterious self-turning atlas. What appears on its pages is a story for another season.
